AMD Ryzen 5 7500 vs 7500F: Price, Specs, and Value in 2026
AMD’s Ryzen 5 7500 is now a released Zen 4 desktop processor, rather than an unconfirmed product rumor. It features six cores, 12 threads, boost speeds of up to 5.0GHz, and basic integrated Radeon graphics. Its key advantage over the Ryzen 5 7500F is that it can provide display output without a dedicated graphics card.

AMD’s suggested US price is $189, although some early retail listings have been higher. That makes the Ryzen 5 7500 worth comparing with the 7500F and Ryzen 5 7600 before buying. Actual value depends on local pricing, whether you need integrated graphics, and the total cost of the PC build.

Nothing here is confirmed. AMD hasn’t announced the chip, no release timing exists, and leaked European pricing has a long history of not matching what retailers actually charge. But the rumor is worth taking seriously for one reason: if that price is real, it would put a slower six-core Zen 4 chip above the Ryzen 7600X3D in some markets, and budget builders should understand exactly what they’d be paying for.
